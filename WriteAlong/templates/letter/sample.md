## Re: Proposal for the renovation of the Reading Room

Dear Ms Jensen,

Thank you for inviting us to visit the Reading Room last week, and for the time you and your colleagues spent showing us around. It is a remarkable space, and your readers clearly love it.

As promised, I enclose our outline proposal. In short, we recommend keeping the room’s character intact while addressing the three issues your team raised: winter heating, lighting at the reading desks, and step-free access from the main hall.

We propose three phases, each completed while the library stays open:

1. **Access** — a ramp and widened doorway from the main hall, built over the summer.
2. **Heating** — secondary glazing to the tall windows and low-temperature underfloor heating.
3. **Lighting** — restored brass desk lamps with warm LED sources, and uplighting for the ceiling.

We estimate the total cost at £185,000–£210,000, depending on the condition of the floor structure; a detailed breakdown is enclosed.

I would be glad to present the proposal to your board in person. Thank you again for considering Northwind for this project.

Yours sincerely,
